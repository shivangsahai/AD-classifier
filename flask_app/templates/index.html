About

Project Overview

Problem

Alzheimer's disease is a progressive neurological disorder where early screening can support faster clinical follow-up.

Approach

The system analyses EEG channel values and transforms them into statistical, energy, asymmetry, and regional features.

Dataset

16 EEG channels are used from frontal, central, temporal, and parietal brain regions.

Output

The model returns a confidence probability mapped into four risk bands with recommendations and a PDF report.

Algorithm

XGBoost Classifier Pipeline

1EEG Input

16 raw channel values are collected manually or through CSV upload.

2Feature Engineering

Mean, variance, skewness, kurtosis, RMS, IQR, energy, ratios, and regional features are calculated.

3Scaling

The saved scaler normalises the engineered features before inference.

4Prediction

The trained model returns the Alzheimer's risk confidence probability.

EEG Guide

Brain Regions and Channels

Frontal

Fp1, Fp2, F3, F4, F7, F8, Fz. Linked with memory, planning, and executive function.

Central

C3, C4, Cz. Related to motor and sensory processing.

Temporal

T3, T4, T5. Important for memory encoding and early cognitive changes.

Parietal

P3, P4, Pz. Associated with attention and spatial awareness.
